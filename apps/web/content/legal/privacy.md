# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [OWNER: effective date]

This policy explains how **EdgeFront**, a sole proprietorship based in Florida, USA ("EdgeFront", "we", "us"), collects, uses and shares personal information when you use getedgefront.com (the "Service").

**In short:**
- You can use the free Service **without an account**.
- If you create an account, we keep your sign-in details and your subscription status.
- We **do not sell** your personal information, **do not show ads** and **do not use advertising or cross-site tracking** cookies.
- Card payments are handled by Link (Stripe), not by us.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information** (only if you create an account):
- your email address and display name;
- your password, which we store only as a scrypt hash, never in readable form;
- if you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address and name, as provided by Google;
- records of which sign-in methods are linked to your account, and when the account was created and last used.

**Subscription information:**
- If you subscribe, Link/Stripe gives us the facts we need to manage your subscription: a customer ID, subscription status, trial and period dates, price, and whether it is set to cancel, the latest invoice status and when you first used the free trial. We do not store your billing address or country. Your card brand and last four digits are read from Link/Stripe to show on your Account page and are not stored by us.
- **We never receive or store your full card number.**

**Technical and security information:**
- When you use the Service, our servers process your IP address, browser user agent, the pages and data requested, and timestamps.
- This is used to deliver pages, keep sessions signed in, apply rate limits and protect against abuse.
- Each sign-in session record stores the IP address and browser user agent it was created from. Rate-limit records are keyed by IP address, or by a one-way hash of your email address or user ID. Server logs can include IP addresses, for example when a request is rate limited. How long each is kept is listed in section 5.

**Communications:** if you email us, we keep your message and our reply.

**Cookies and local storage:**
- We use **essential cookies only**, to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in forms.
- The cookies are `better-auth.session_token` (keeps you signed in; expires 30 days after you last use the site; named `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` on HTTPS) and `better-auth.state` (5 minutes, only while you sign in with Google). In your browser's local storage we keep one key, `better-auth.message`, which tells your other open EdgeFront tabs when you sign in or out; it contains no personal information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies.

We do **not** collect sensitive personal information such as government IDs, precise location, health or biometric data.

## 2. How we use information
We use personal information to:
- provide the Service, your account and Premium access;
- check your subscription status (entitlement) and manage billing with Link/Stripe;
- send **transactional emails**, such as verification, password reset, trial and billing notices and important changes to the Service. We do not send marketing emails;
- keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse (including more than one free trial per account), and enforce our Terms;
- fix problems and understand overall usage in aggregate;
- comply with law, tax and accounting obligations.

**Legal bases (EEA/UK users):**
- performance of our contract with you (account and Premium);
- our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the Service);
- compliance with legal obligations;
- consent, where we ask for it.

## 3. Google user data
If you sign in with Google, we use only your basic profile data (name, email address and Google account ID), and only to create your account and sign you in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

EdgeFront's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it except as needed to provide the Service.

## 4. Who we share information with
We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of it, and as described below.

| Provider | What they do | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Railway | Hosts our website, servers and database (United States, US-East region) | All account, subscription and technical data we store |
| Amazon Web Services | Stores encrypted database backups (United States, US-East region) | Database backups |
| Link / Stripe | Payments, tax, invoices, receipts, billing support; acts as **merchant of record** under its own privacy policy | Payment details you give them; your email; subscription data |
| Resend | Sends our transactional emails | Your email address and the email content |
| Google | Sign-in with Google (if you choose it) | The sign-in exchange |
| OpenFreeMap | Map tiles on the Premium map | Your IP address and browser info when your browser loads map tiles |

**Link (Stripe) uses your payment information as an independent controller under the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).**

We may also disclose information:
- if required by law, subpoena or court order;
- to protect the rights, safety and security of EdgeFront, our users or others;
- in connection with a sale or transfer of the business. In that case we will notify you, and the new owner must honor this policy.

**We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

## 5. How long we keep information
- **Account data:** until you delete your account. You can do this at any time in **Account**.
- **Sessions:** expire after up to 30 days without use. Expired sessions are deleted within 7 days after they expire.
- **Security and server logs:** up to 90 days, at our host Railway.
- **Rate-limit records:** up to 1 day.
- **Backups:** deleted data stays in encrypted backups until they rotate out, within 56 days.
- **Billing records:** subscription records may be kept as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal purposes. Link/Stripe keeps its own records under its policy.

## 6. Security
- We use reasonable safeguards: HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, restricted access to production systems, and private networking between our services.
- No system is perfectly secure.
- If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires. Under Florida law, this means within 30 days of determining that a breach occurred, unless a lawful delay applies.

## 7. Your choices and rights
- **Access and export:** ask us for a copy of your personal information.
- **Correction:** update your name and email in **Account**, or ask us.
- **Deletion:** delete your account in **Account**, or ask us.
- **Email:** you can't opt out of transactional emails while you have an account, because they're needed for the Service. We don't send marketing emails.

To make a request, email [OWNER: support email] from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

**EEA/UK users** also have the right to:
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- data portability;
- withdraw consent;
- complain to your local data-protection authority.

**U.S. state privacy laws:** depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information. You can use them as described above, whether or not your state's law applies to a business of our size.

## 8. International users
We are based in the United States, and we store and process data in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

## 9. Children
The Service is **not for anyone under 18**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control
We don't track users across other websites and don't sell or share personal information for advertising. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals therefore don't change how we handle your data.
