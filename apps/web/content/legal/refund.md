# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** [OWNER: effective date]

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). It applies to the EdgeFront Premium subscription.

## Free trial
- New subscribers get a **7-day free trial**. A payment card is required to start it.
- **If you don't cancel before the trial ends, you will automatically be charged $20.00 plus applicable tax on the day the trial ends, and every month after that until you cancel.**
- We email you a reminder before the trial ends (3 days before it ends).
- If you cancel during the trial, you will not be charged.
- The free trial is available **once per account**.

## Cancel anytime
You can cancel online at any time. You don't need to call or email anyone.
1. Sign in and go to **Account → Manage billing**.
2. Choose **Cancel subscription** and confirm.

You can also cancel from your **Link account** at link.com.

**What happens after you cancel:**
- Your cancellation takes effect at the **end of your current paid period**.
- You keep Premium until then.
- You **won't be charged again**.
- You'll receive a confirmation email from EdgeFront with the date your Premium ends.

## Refunds
Subscription payments are **non-refundable**, including for partial months, unused time, or if you forget to cancel. The exceptions are:
1. **Billing errors.** We will refund duplicate charges, or charges for the wrong amount.
2. **Charges after you cancelled.** If you were charged after cancelling because of an error on our side or the payment processor's, we will refund that charge.
3. **Where the law requires it.** If you are a consumer in a country whose law gives you a right to cancel or a refund, nothing in this policy limits that right.
4. **If we stop offering Premium.** If we discontinue Premium, we will refund the prepaid, unused part of your current period.

We may also give a refund or credit at our discretion in other cases, for example after a long outage. Doing so once does not mean we will do it again.

## Payments through Link
- Payments are processed by **Link**, a service of Stripe, which is the merchant of record for your purchase.
- Your statement may show **LINK.COM\* EDGEFRONT**.
- You can also contact Link support about billing.
- Link may process refund requests for your purchase under its own [Consumer Terms](https://link.com/terms).
- Refunds go back to your original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## How to ask for a refund
Email [OWNER: support email] with:
- the email address on your account;
- the date and amount of the charge;
- why you think it should be refunded.

We reply within **2 business days**.

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. Most billing problems can be fixed quickly.

## Price changes
- We will email you **at least 30 days before** a price change applies to your subscription.
- If you don't want to continue at the new price, cancel before the change takes effect and you won't be charged the new price.
